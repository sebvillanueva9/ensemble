# Ensemble

Ensemble keeps two people's streaming players in sync so they can watch the same movie or series together from different countries.

## Language

**Partner**:
One of the exactly two people using Ensemble. Both Partners have equal control.
_Avoid_: Host, guest, leader, follower, user

**Watch Session**:
The span during which both Partners' players are linked and kept in sync. It starts when a Partner accepts an Invite, continues across episode changes and Drops, and ends when either Partner Leaves or a dropped Partner doesn't come back in time.
_Avoid_: Party, room, call

**Pair**:
The permanent link between the two Partners' copies of Ensemble, made once and kept until either Partner unpairs.
_Avoid_: Account, connection, room

**Invite**:
A Partner's request for the other Partner to join a Watch Session on a given title, delivered in Ensemble or as a link.
_Avoid_: Party link, session request

**Leave**:
A Partner deliberately ending the Watch Session (by the Leave control or by going back to browsing).
_Avoid_: Quit, disconnect

**Drop**:
A Partner unintentionally falling out of a Watch Session (lost connection, closed tab, closed or crashed browser), from which they can come back without a new Invite.
_Avoid_: Leave, disconnect

**Playback Command**:
A play, pause, or seek issued by either Partner and mirrored on the other Partner's player.
_Avoid_: Action, control event

**Drift**:
The difference between the playback positions of the two Partners' players.
_Avoid_: Lag, offset, desync

**Resync**:
A correction that brings Drift back within the acceptable limit.
_Avoid_: Realign, catch-up
